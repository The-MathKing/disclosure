# Stimulus Texts and Survey Items

## 1. Stimulus Texts

We manipulated two factors: Product Type (Search Good vs. Experience Good) and AI Disclosure (None, AI-assisted, AI-generated). 

### Base Review 1: Search Good (USB Flash Drive)
> "I recently picked up the 256GB OmniDrive flash drive to back up my work files. Out of the box, it feels sturdy with a solid metal casing. Transfer speeds are exactly as advertised—I moved a 10GB folder of photos and videos in under a minute without any thermal throttling. The retractable design is a nice touch, so I don’t have to worry about losing a cap. It formatted seamlessly on both my Mac and Windows machines. Overall, it’s a reliable and fast storage solution for a reasonable price." (89 words)

### Base Review 2: Experience Good (Facial Serum)
> "I recently picked up the Radiance Botanical facial serum to add to my nighttime routine. Out of the box, it feels luxurious with a subtle, calming lavender scent. Application is incredibly smooth—it absorbs into the skin in under a minute without leaving any sticky or greasy residue. The hydrating formula is a nice touch, and I haven't had to worry about it irritating my sensitive skin. It layered seamlessly under my standard moisturizer. Overall, it leaves my skin feeling rejuvenated and glowing for a reasonable price." (86 words)

## 2. Survey Measures

### Product Typology Manipulation Check (Nelson 1970 adapted)
Please rate the product described in the review on the following scale:
- 1 = Its quality can easily be determined before purchasing (Search Good)
- 7 = Its quality can only be determined by personally using or experiencing it (Experience Good)

### Perceived AI Involvement Manipulation Check
How much do you believe Artificial Intelligence (AI) was involved in writing this review?
- 1 = Completely written by a human
- 4 = Moderately assisted by AI
- 7 = Completely generated by AI

### Source Authenticity (Adapted)
Rate your agreement with the following statements (1 = Strongly Disagree, 7 = Strongly Agree):
1. The author of this review seems genuine.
2. The review reflects a real, authentic experience.
3. I believe the reviewer actually used the product.

### Consumer Trust (McKnight et al. 2002 adapted)
Rate your agreement with the following statements (1 = Strongly Disagree, 7 = Strongly Agree):
1. I believe this review is reliable.
2. I trust the information provided in this review.
3. This review is a credible source of information about the product.

### Purchase Intention (Putrevu & Lord 1994)
Based on this review, how likely would you be to consider purchasing this product?
- 1 = Unlikely / 7 = Likely
- 1 = Improbable / 7 = Probable
- 1 = Impossible / 7 = Possible

### Data Quality & Attention Checks
1. **Attention Check 1 (embedded in Trust scale):** "To ensure you are reading carefully, please select 'Strongly Disagree' for this item."
2. **Attention Check 2 (standalone):** "Which product was being evaluated in the review you just read?" [USB Flash Drive / Facial Serum / Scented Candle / Phone Charger]
3. **Open-text Bot Screen:** "In 1-2 sentences, what was the most persuasive part of the review you read?" *(Responses will be manually screened for generic LLM boilerplate such as 'As an AI, I do not have feelings' or extreme low effort).*
