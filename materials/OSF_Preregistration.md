# OSF Pre-registration: The Effect of AI Disclosure on Consumer Trust

## 1. Data collection
Have any data been collected for this study already?
- [x] No, no data have been collected for this study yet.

## 2. Hypothesis
What's the main question being asked or hypothesis being tested in this study?
- **H1 (Main Effect):** Higher levels of AI involvement disclosure (None > AI-assisted > AI-generated) will lead to lower perceived source authenticity, consumer trust, and purchase intention.
- **H2 (Interaction):** Product type moderates the effect of AI disclosure on perceived authenticity. The negative effect of AI disclosure (vs. no disclosure) will be stronger for experience goods (e.g., facial serum) than for search goods (e.g., USB flash drive).
- **H3 (Moderated Mediation):** The interactive effect of AI disclosure and product type on purchase intention is mediated by perceived source authenticity.

## 3. Dependent variable
Describe the key dependent variable(s) specifying how they will be measured.
- **Perceived Source Authenticity**: Measured via adapted items.
- **Consumer Trust**: Measured via adapted McKnight et al. (2002) trust scale.
- **Purchase Intention**: Measured via Putrevu & Lord (1994) semantic differential (likely/unlikely, probable/improbable, possible/impossible).

## 4. Conditions
How many and which conditions will participants be assigned to?
The study uses a 3 (AI Disclosure: None vs. AI-assisted vs. AI-generated) × 2 (Product Type: Search good [USB flash drive] vs. Experience good [Facial serum]) between-subjects design. Participants will be randomly assigned to one of the 6 conditions.

## 5. Analyses
Specify exactly which analyses you will conduct to examine the main question/hypothesis.
- **Manipulation Check**: Independent samples t-test to confirm the search/experience good manipulation.
- **Main Analysis**: Two-way ANOVA to test the main effects and interaction effect of AI Disclosure and Product Type on the dependent variables.
- **Moderated Mediation**: Hayes PROCESS macro (Model 7 or 8) with multicategorical independent variable (AI Disclosure) to test if Perceived Source Authenticity mediates the moderated effect on Purchase Intention.

## 6. Outliers and Exclusions
Describe exactly how outliers will be defined and handled, and your precise rule(s) for excluding observations.
- Participants who fail the attention checks (2-3 items) will be excluded.
- Participants flagged as bots or providing low-effort, AI-generated open-text responses will be excluded.
- Participants with unusually fast completion times (e.g., < 30% of median time) will be flagged and excluded.

## 7. Sample Size
How many observations will be collected or what will determine sample size? No need to justify decision, but be precise about exactly how the number will be determined.
Target sample size is N = 252 (42 per condition). We will recruit up to 300 gross participants on Prolific to account for exclusions due to failed attention checks.

## 8. Other
Anything else you would like to pre-register?
We will run robustness checks controlling for participant age, gender, and AI familiarity.
