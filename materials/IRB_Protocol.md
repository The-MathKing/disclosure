# IRB Exemption Protocol: AI Disclosure in Consumer Reviews

## 1. Study Title
The Effect of AI Disclosure Levels on Consumer Trust: A Search vs. Experience Good Typology

## 2. Principal Investigator
[User Name/Aryan Padarthi]

## 3. Abstract
This research investigates how disclosing the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in writing product reviews impacts consumer trust, perceived source authenticity, and purchase intentions. Specifically, we examine whether the effect differs depending on whether the product is a "search good" (e.g., USB flash drive) or an "experience good" (e.g., facial serum). The study uses a 3x2 between-subjects experimental design administered online via Prolific and Qualtrics/SoSci Survey.

## 4. Subject Population
- **Target N**: 300 participants.
- **Source**: Prolific (crowdsourcing platform).
- **Inclusion Criteria**: 18 years or older, English fluency, Prolific approval rate ≥ 95%.
- **Exclusion Criteria**: Under 18 years of age.

## 5. Procedures
Participants will be recruited via Prolific and directed to an online survey hosted on Qualtrics/SoSci Survey. 
1. Participants will read an informed consent form and must agree to participate before proceeding.
2. They will be randomly assigned to read one of six product reviews (manipulating product type and AI disclosure level).
3. After reading the review, participants will answer questions regarding their perceived authenticity of the reviewer, trust in the review, and purchase intention.
4. Participants will answer standard demographic questions and attention checks.
5. Upon completion, participants will be compensated via Prolific at a standard rate of ~$12/hour.

## 6. Risks and Benefits
- **Risks**: This study involves minimal risk. The procedures involve reading a brief product review and answering survey questions. There is no physical, psychological, social, or legal risk beyond what is encountered in everyday life.
- **Benefits**: There is no direct benefit to the participants other than financial compensation. The research will benefit society by informing transparent marketing practices and regulatory guidelines regarding AI-generated content.

## 7. Confidentiality and Data Security
No identifying information (e.g., names, email addresses, IP addresses) will be collected or linked to the survey responses. Prolific IDs will be used solely for compensation purposes and will be separated from the main dataset before analysis. Data will be stored securely on encrypted, password-protected computers and shared publicly only in a fully de-identified format via OSF.

## 8. Informed Consent
Participants will be presented with an online informed consent document at the beginning of the survey. They must click a button indicating their understanding and agreement to participate before they can access the study materials. (See attached Informed Consent document).

## 9. Request for Exemption
This research qualifies for exemption under 45 CFR 46.104(d)(2): Research that only includes interactions involving educational tests, survey procedures, interview procedures, or observation of public behavior, where the information obtained is recorded by the investigator in such a manner that the identity of the human subjects cannot readily be ascertained, directly or through identifiers linked to the subjects.
