# Informed Consent

**Study Title:** Consumer Perceptions of Product Reviews

**Purpose of the Study:** 
You are invited to participate in a research study investigating how consumers perceive and evaluate online product reviews. 

**Procedures:** 
If you choose to participate, you will be asked to read a short product review and answer a series of questions regarding your opinions and impressions of the review and the product. The survey will take approximately 4-6 minutes to complete.

**Risks and Benefits:** 
There are no known risks or discomforts associated with this study beyond those encountered in everyday life. While there is no direct benefit to you, your participation will help researchers better understand consumer behavior in digital environments.

**Compensation:** 
Upon successful completion of the survey, you will receive compensation as advertised on Prolific. Please note that you must pass attention checks to have your submission approved.

**Confidentiality:** 
Your responses will be kept completely confidential and anonymous. We do not collect any personally identifying information. Your Prolific ID will be used solely for the purpose of distributing compensation and will be separated from your survey responses before data analysis. The de-identified data may be shared in public research repositories.

**Voluntary Participation:** 
Your participation in this study is entirely voluntary. You may choose to withdraw from the study at any time before submitting your responses without penalty. 

**Contact Information:** 
If you have any questions or concerns about this research, please contact the Principal Investigator via the Prolific messaging system.
